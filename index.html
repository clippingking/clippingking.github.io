Clipping King

A Mac app that turns long videos into short clips and publishes them to your own accounts.

What it does

Clipping King helps a video creator find the strongest moments in a long video, such as a podcast episode, and prepare them as vertical clips. It runs on your own Mac.

  1. Add a videoChoose a long video you have the right to use, for example one approved for a clipping campaign.
  2. Get clip suggestionsThe app transcribes the video and uses AI to suggest complete, self-contained moments, then adds captions and vertical framing.
  3. Review each clipYou watch each clip, edit its caption and approve it. Nothing is posted without your approval.
  4. PublishApproved clips are published to the accounts you connected, including TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. For TikTok, you review the connected account and caption and confirm before anything is posted.
  5. Track resultsYou can see the post link and its status, and submit the link to a clipping campaign you joined.

Connecting TikTok

You sign in through TikTok’s own login page and choose whether to grant access. Clipping King uses that access to read your basic profile, so it can show which account is connected, and to publish the videos you approve. It does not read your messages, your followers or your other videos.

Your data

Clipping King has no user accounts of its own, and this website collects nothing. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
