Clipping King Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy explains how the Clipping King Mac app (“Clipping King”) and this website handle your information.

This website

This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver it.

Information the app handles

Clipping King runs on your Mac. Its developer does not operate a server that receives your videos, account details or login tokens.

  • Connected account details: when you connect TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the app receives login tokens and your basic profile, such as your display name. TikTok access is limited to the user.info.basic and video.publish permissions.
  • Your videos and captions: the videos, transcripts and captions you work on are stored on your Mac.
  • Publishing history: the app stores post links, statuses and performance statistics for your posts on your Mac.

How information is used

The app uses this information only to show which account is connected, to publish the clips you approve, and to show results. It does not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where information is sent

  • Social platforms: clips and captions you approve are sent to the platform you chose, such as TikTok. That platform’s own privacy policy applies.
  • AI services: when an online AI model is configured, video transcripts and clip text are sent to that provider, currently OpenAI, to suggest and review clips.
  • Download and campaign services: if you import a video by link or submit a post link to a campaign, the app contacts those services.

Storage and security

Login tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. Other data stays in the app’s local files until you delete it.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account by removing the app’s access in that platform’s settings. In TikTok, this is under your security and permissions settings. You can delete local data by removing the app and its files from your Mac.

Children

Clipping King is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will appear on this page.

Contact

Questions can be sent through this contact page.
